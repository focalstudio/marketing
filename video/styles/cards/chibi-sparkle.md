## Look
Kawaii-chibi, pushed cuter: the head carries two thirds of the figure and the eyes take half the head, dark with a lighter iris and two white highlights. A tiny mouth, pink blush, one soft plum outline. Sparkles and hearts twinkle over a pale pink page patterned with hearts.

## Do
- Keep the eyes huge, glossy, with a big and a small highlight.
- Use pastel flats only: pink, mint, lilac, a touch of gold.
- Scatter sparkles and hearts, each twinkling on its own beat.

## Don't
- No gradients: the gloss is a white streak, not a blend.
- No dark or saturated backgrounds, no thick black outlines.

## Prompt recipe
Cute chibi kawaii, soft plum outline, head two thirds of the body, huge glossy eyes with two highlights, tiny mouth, blush, sparkles and hearts, flat pastel page with a heart pattern, Nunito type. Subject: {subject}, 9:16.
