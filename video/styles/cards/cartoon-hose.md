## Look
Constant-width cream tubes in a heavy black outline for the limbs (no gloves), a round cream rabbit with droopy lop ears and a black bow, pie-cut eyes. The rabbit, the timer, the ears and each title letter squash on the same 80 bpm beat. A film layer redrawn at 24 frames a second adds grain, flicker, gate weave, scratches, dust and a vignette.

## Do
- Bounce every object on the beat, with the ears and title letters a little late.
- Keep to black, cream and their tints.

## Don't
- No joints, no gradients and no colour.
- No resemblance to any specific studio's characters.
