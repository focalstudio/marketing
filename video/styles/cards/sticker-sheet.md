## Look
Every piece is a sticker: its art inside one plum outline, a thick white die-cut border that follows the silhouette, a soft drop shadow underneath and a bright gloss streak across it. They lie on a dark indigo page a few degrees off square, as if just peeled and pressed down.

## Do
- Give every piece the same border width, white, following its outline.
- Tilt each sticker a few degrees and let the shadow fall down and right.
- Let the gloss streak cross each sticker at the same angle.

## Don't
- No sticker without its white border, and no border on the page itself.
- No gradients inside the art: the gloss is one flat translucent band.

## Prompt recipe
Die-cut vinyl stickers on a dark page: thick white offset border, soft drop shadow, glossy highlight streak, slight tilt, plum outlines, Nunito 900 lettering. Subject: {subject}, 9:16.
