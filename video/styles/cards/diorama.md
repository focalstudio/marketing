## Look
A toy-sized scene on a wooden base, seen from a low angle at close range. A huge aperture blurs the foreground and background, and lamps in a dark room turn into soft bokeh discs. Saturated glossy plastic sells the scale.

## Do
- Put the focus on the subject and let the base edge and background blur.
- Use glossy, saturated plastic materials with a clear coat.
- Add warm lamps far behind to make bokeh.

## Don't
- No sharp backgrounds or flat even light.
- No realistic textures: every part looks moulded.

## Prompt recipe
Miniature diorama, glossy toy plastic on a wooden base, low camera, very shallow depth of field, bokeh lamps. Subject: {subject}, 9:16.
