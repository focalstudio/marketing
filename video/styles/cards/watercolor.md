## Look
Every shape is a few blurred, translucent copies glazed on top of each other, with a darker rim where the pigment pooled. Blooms lighten patches of the sky and meadow, grit shows the paper through the colour, and a white masked border frames the sheet.

## Do
- Let washes overlap and bleed into each other.
- Keep the palette pastel: sage, mint, butter yellow, one soft pink.
- Leave paper showing, and add a few splatters.

## Don't
- No hard outlines or black.
- No flat vector fills or perfect circles.

## Prompt recipe
Wet-on-wet watercolour on cream paper, soft bleeding edges, pooled pigment rims, blooms, visible paper grain, a few splatters, pastel greens and warm yellow. Subject: {subject}, 9:16.
