## Look
Fills are rows of wavy wax streaks with paper showing between them, laid a little past the outline. Outlines are heavy, uneven in pressure and drawn twice, overshooting where they close. A speckled paper tooth sits over everything.

## Do
- Stay with red, blue, yellow and green, plus orange for the sun.
- Let colour spill outside the lines and leave blank paper.
- Scribble where a child would scribble: the sun, shadows, petals.

## Don't
- No smooth gradients, blur or clean vector edges.
- No fine detail: nothing thinner than the crayon tip.

## Prompt recipe
Children's wax crayon drawing on white paper, thick wobbly outlines, streaky fills past the lines, paper tooth through the wax, saturated primaries, a scribbled sun. Subject: {subject}, 9:16.
