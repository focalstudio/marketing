## Look
A round rabbit built from a few ovals, each shape in two flat tones: a light face and a shade crescent on the lower right. No outline, no gradient. Sage and cream carry the page, ink the type, one sun orange for the accent. Fredoka for every word.

## Do
- Build the character from circles and ovals only.
- Give every shape exactly two tones.
- Keep type in Fredoka, 500 to 700 weight, warm ink on cream.

## Don't
- No outlines, gradients or textures.
- No third tone inside a shape: shine and shadow are the same crescent.

## Prompt recipe
Flat vector mascot, no outlines, two flat tones per shape, Fredoka rounded type, sage green and cream with one sun-orange accent. Subject: {subject}, 9:16.
