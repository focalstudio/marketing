## Look
Clean Bezier and ellipse shapes, no boil and no texture. All parts of a bunny are stroked and filled plum, then filled white on top, so overlapping parts merge into one silhouette outline. Pastel pages change with each voice line.

## Do
- Draw one outline around the whole silhouette.
- Put one keyword on screen per voice line.
- Change the page colour from the pastel set.

## Don't
- No texture, grain or gradients.
- No second outline colour.

## Prompt recipe
