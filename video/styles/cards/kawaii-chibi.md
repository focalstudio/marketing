## Look
Flat, rounded stickers on white paper: chunky shapes, bold dark outlines, a flat shade block instead of gradients, 3D-lip buttons and one saturated green for progress and headings. Squash and stretch on every hop.

## Do
- Give characters a head bigger than the body.
- Use the green only for progress and headings.
- Squash on landing, stretch on the way up.

## Don't
- No gradients or textures.
- No thin lines: the outline is `LOOK.out` wide everywhere.

## Prompt recipe
Kawaii chibi sticker, huge head, tiny body, bold dark outline, flat shade block, white paper, one saturated green accent. Subject: {subject}, 9:16.
