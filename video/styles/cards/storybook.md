## Look
Smooth rolling hills and round-crowned trees in six warm colours. The cast has thin darker outlines and moves smoothly at 24 poses a second. A paper grain sits over every frame.

## Do
- Stay inside the six-colour palette plus warm light.
- Keep outlines thin and a shade darker than the fill.
- Print the grain over the whole frame, text included.

## Don't
- No hard black.
- No shadows or gradients inside shapes.

## Prompt recipe
Flat vector picture-book illustration, warm limited palette, thin darker outlines, round trees, paper grain over everything. Subject: {subject}, 9:16.
