## Look
Puffy rounded felt shapes with a fine fibre grain and a soft halo at the edges. Contrasting thread dashes run along seams, ears, the island rim and the timer border. The background is one more sheet of felt.

## Do
- Add a mottle and fibre bump so flat colour never looks plastic.
- Stitch the visible seams with short thread dashes.
- Keep shapes simple, puffy and slightly oversized.

## Don't
- No glossy highlights or hard outlines.
- No fine detail the felt could not be cut from.

## Prompt recipe
Felt craft, wool felt with fibre texture, stitched seams in contrasting thread, felt backdrop, soft light. Subject: {subject}, 9:16.
