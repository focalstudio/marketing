# See you tomorrow: the prompt behind the clip

An 18-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It sells the end of the day: you finish, close the app, and the meadow winds down until tomorrow.

- **0–3.6 s, hook.** "Done for today?" pops in over a daytime meadow with a deer, a hedgehog, a fox and a rabbit.
- **0.5–9 s, evening.** The light tints from day to dusk to night and the stars come out.
- **4.4–10.4 s, going home.** One by one the animals wander off the edges of the frame: the deer, the hedgehog, the fox, and the rabbit last, hopping. Each goodbye is a soft chime.
- **10.4–13.8 s, payoff.** The meadow is quiet. "They'll be here tomorrow."
- **14.5–18 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." with the rabbit below.
- **Rules.** Drawn styles only (`paper`, `storybook`; the retired `appart` pasted the app's static PNGs). The icon is always the real one.
