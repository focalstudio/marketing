# Night owls: the prompt behind the clip

A 17-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. For the people studying at 2 a.m.

- **0–3.6 s, hook.** "Late night study session?" over a rabbit in the meadow at dusk.
- **0.5–8 s, nightfall.** The sky tints from dusk to deep blue and the stars come out one by one.
- **8.5–10 s, the owl.** An owl glides in from the top right and lands next to the rabbit, with a chime.
- **10.5–13 s, the bat.** A bat flits across the frame in short hops. The copy reads "You've got company."
- **14–17 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." with the rabbit below.
- **Rules.** Appart style only. The icon is always the real one.
