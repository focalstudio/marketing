# Short break: the prompt behind the clip

An 18-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It sells the gentle side of the app: the break is as calm as the focus.

- **0–3 s, hook.** "Break time." pops in over the meadow.
- **1–9 s, the break.** A 5:00 timer runs down. An otter glides in from the left, a frog hops in from the right, and a hedgehog pops up in the grass, each with a soft chime.
- **10.2–13.6 s, the payoff.** "Rest is part of the work." rises over the settled meadow.
- **14.5–18 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." with the otter below.
- **Rules.** Drawn styles only (`paper`, `storybook`; the retired `appart` pasted the app's static PNGs). The icon is always the real one.
