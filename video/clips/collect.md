# Collect them all: the prompt behind the clip

An 18-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It sells the loop: focus, and the meadow fills up.

- **0–3 s, hook.** "Every 25 minutes of focus…" pops in over an empty meadow.
- **1–11 s, the loop.** A 25:00 timer pill runs down to 00:00 three times. At each zero an animal pops in (rabbit, fox, hedgehog) with a chime that rises each time.
- **12–14 s, payoff.** "…someone new moves in."
- **14.5–18 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS."
- **Variants.** `meadow` (rabbit, fox, hedgehog), `woodland` (squirrel, owl, mouse), `pond` (duck, otter, frog).
- **Sound.** Synthesised wind plus sine chimes. No music, no voice.
- **Rules.** Appart style only: the app's own backdrop and animal PNGs, nothing redrawn. The icon is always the real one.
