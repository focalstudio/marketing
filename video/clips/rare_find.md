# Rare find: the prompt behind the clip

A 19-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It sells the long game: stick with it and something rare shows up.

- **0–3 s, hook.** "Seven days of focus." pops in over the meadow.
- **0.6–8.4 s, the week.** A "Day 1/7" pill ticks up every 1.3 s. A small animal pops in on days 2–6 (mouse, frog, duck, hedgehog, squirrel), each chime a step higher.
- **8.4–10.4 s, the pause.** Day 7. Nothing moves, and the light warms to dusk.
- **10.4–13.2 s, the reveal.** A rare animal slowly rises from the grass in the middle of the meadow. A two-note chime. "Then this showed up."
- **15.6–19 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." with the rare animal below.
- **Variants.** `snow_leopard`, `polar_bear`, `red_panda`, `tiger`.
- **Rules.** Appart style only. The icon is always the real one.
