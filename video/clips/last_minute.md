# Last minute: the prompt behind the clip

An 18-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It sells the last stretch of a session: stay for it and the meadow keeps you company.

- **0–3 s, hook.** "One minute left." pops in over an empty meadow.
- **1–9 s, the countdown.** A 1:00 timer pill runs down to 00:00. A goose glides in, a deer rises from behind the ridge and a sheep pops up beside them, with chimes that rise each time.
- **9–10.2 s, the zero.** A final chime and a gust of leaves.
- **10.2–13.6 s, payoff.** "Stay for the last bit."
- **14.5–18 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." with the deer below.
- **Sound.** Synthesised wind plus sine chimes. No music, no voice.
- **Rules.** Drawn styles only (`paper`, `storybook`; the retired `appart` pasted the app's static PNGs). The icon is always the real one.
