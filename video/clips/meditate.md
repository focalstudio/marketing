# Meditating rabbit: the prompt behind the clip

A 16-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It should calm the viewer and then make them want the app.

- **0–3 s, hook.** "Stop scrolling." pops in, then "Breathe." The rabbit is already sitting cross-legged on a rock in a golden-hour meadow, eyes closed.
- **3–9 s, meditation.** The camera slowly pushes in on the rabbit, which breathes on a 3-second cycle. Around 6 s a gust of wind blows its ears sideways, bends the grass and blows a stream of autumn leaves across the frame. The rabbit stays calm.
- **9–13 s, friends.** The camera pulls back out. A fox walks in from the left, and a deer and a hedgehog from the right. Each one sits down next to the rabbit, gives a small bounce when it lands, then blinks and tilts its head toward the rabbit. A soft chime plays as each one sits. The rabbit opens its eyes and smiles. The copy reads "…and make a new friend."
- **13–16 s, end card.** A short dissolve, then the exact app icon drops in with a bounce and two chimes. "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." fade in, with the rabbit still meditating, smaller, below them.
- **Sound.** Synthesised brown-noise wind that swells with the gust, plus sine chimes. No music and no voice.
- **Rules.** Everything is drawn in code except the app icon, which is always the real one and is never redrawn or stylised. The timings, copy and chimes are the same in every style.

Rendered so far in toon (flat cartoon after *The Amazing World of Gumball*), low poly (the app's origami look), pixel (16-bit, 135×240 ×8) and ink.
