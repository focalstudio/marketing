# Leaf fall: the prompt behind the clip

An 18-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It sells the mood: slow, seasonal, the opposite of a feed.

- **0–3 s, hook.** "Leaves fall. You stay." pops in over the meadow.
- **1–10 s, the session.** A sheep sits still while a 25:00 timer runs down and leaves drift past. A squirrel peeks out of the grass and a goose glides in to watch, each with a soft chime.
- **10.4–13.6 s, payoff.** "Nothing to chase. Just the season."
- **14.5–18 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." with the sheep below.
- **Sound.** Synthesised wind plus sine chimes. No music, no voice.
- **Rules.** Drawn styles only (`paper`, `storybook`; the retired `appart` pasted the app's static PNGs). The icon is always the real one.
