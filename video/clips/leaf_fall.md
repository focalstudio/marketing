# Leaf fall: the prompt behind the clip

A 19-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It sells the unhurried feel: an autumn gust passes, the timer keeps running, and nothing is bothered.

- **0–3.4 s, hook.** "Leaves fall. Timer runs." pops in over a red panda sitting in the meadow.
- **1–10.4 s, the session.** One 25:00 timer runs down. A gust at 2.2 s sends autumn leaves drifting across. A squirrel hops in, a goose glides over and a hedgehog peeks up from the grass, each with a soft chime.
- **10.8–14.4 s, the payoff.** The timer ends on a two-note chime. "Let it fall. Keep going."
- **15.5–19 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." with the red panda below.
- **Rules.** Drawn styles only (`paper`, `storybook`; the retired `appart` pasted the app's static PNGs). The icon is always the real one.
