# Slow lane: the prompt behind the clip

An 18-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It sells the calm: nothing in the meadow is in a hurry, and you don't need to be either.

- **0–3 s, hook.** "No rush." pops in over the meadow.
- **1–10 s, the pace.** A sea turtle glides slowly across the grass. A rabbit and a squirrel hop in fast, a mouse scurries after them, and each settles beside the turtle with a soft chime.
- **10.4–14 s, payoff.** "Take your time. They'll wait."
- **14.5–18 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." with the sea turtle below.
- **Sound.** Synthesised wind plus sine chimes. No music, no voice.
- **Rules.** Drawn styles only (`paper`, `storybook`; the retired `appart` pasted the app's static PNGs). The icon is always the real one.
