# Stop scrolling: the prompt behind the clip

A 16-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) silent promo for WildFocus. The style is cream paper, pencil hatching, wobbly ink outlines and a hand-drawn sun, after the Claude ant film.

- **0–2.9 s, hook.** Plain paper with a hand-drawn sun. "Stop scrolling." pops in big, then "Breathe. Watch this." appears in sage.
- **2.9–9.2 s, hops.** A drawn landscape: hills, pines, a pond and a dirt strip, with a very slow push-in. The rabbit from the app icon, holding a carrot, hops three times to the right and three times back. It squashes when it lands, has speed lines at the top of each hop, and its shadow shrinks as it rises.
- **9.2–13 s, focus.** Dusk falls: the sky goes to night with stars and the sun sinks. A focus ring fills from 00:00 to 25:00 around the rabbit. The fox, the deer and the hedgehog pop in one after another. The copy reads "25 minutes of focus earns you a friend."
- **13–16 s, end card.** The exact app icon drops in with a soft blurred shadow, then "WildFocus", "Free on iOS." and "Phone down. Rabbit up."
- **Rules.** Everything is drawn in code except the app icon. The outlines "boil": four seeded variants of every sprite are cycled every 3 frames.
