# Golden hour: the prompt behind the clip

A 20-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. It sells the unhurried side of the app: one focus session as the day winds down, with quiet company.

- **0–3.2 s, hook.** "One session. One evening." pops in over the meadow.
- **1–11 s, the session.** A 25:00 timer runs down while the light slowly turns to dusk. A goat walks in from the left, a chicken hops in from the right, a sheep pops up in the grass and a goose glides in, each with a soft chime.
- **12–16 s, the payoff.** "Let the day wind down." rises over the settled meadow with a two-note chime.
- **16.6–20 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS." with the goat below.
- **Rules.** Drawn styles only (`paper`, `storybook`; the retired `appart` pasted the app's static PNGs). The icon is always the real one.
