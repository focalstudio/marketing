# Phone down: the prompt behind the clip

An 18-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) promo for WildFocus, with sound. The phone is the thing in the way.

- **0–3 s, hook.** "Phone down." pops in, then "Wait." A phone lies face-down in the grass.
- **1.6–6 s, they come.** A deer and a hedgehog peek up from the grass. A rabbit and a fox creep in from the sides.
- **6.2–7.6 s, buzz.** The phone buzzes and shakes. Every animal freezes, trembling.
- **7.6–9 s, quiet.** It goes still. The animals finish creeping and settle around it, with soft chimes.
- **9–13 s, payoff.** "They only come out when you put it down."
- **14–18 s, end card.** Dissolve, the real app icon drops in with two chimes, "WildFocus" and "Free on iOS."
- **Variants.** `meadow` (deer, hedgehog, fox, rabbit) and `pond` (otter, mouse, duck, frog).
- **Rules.** Appart style only. The icon is always the real one.
