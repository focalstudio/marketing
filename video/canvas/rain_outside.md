# Rain outside: the prompt behind the video

A 30-second vertical video (1080×1920, 30 fps; `--size 1080x1080` for square) for WildFocus. It's drawn in canvas code
as a naturalist engraving, after the "life of a fruit fly" that Opus drew in JavaScript: cream paper, ink outlines,
pen hatching, watercolour washes, diagonal bands of light and a specimen ruler. No animals. It sells the
feeling of switching off, not the app. One drop's life, with the camera following it:

- **0–3 s, hook.** Extreme close-up of one drop on the window, with the garden upside down inside it. The text is there
  from the first frame: "You don't have to do anything for 30 seconds." The rain is heard at full level at once.
- **3–8 s.** The camera rises into the cloud. Droplets drift together into one drop, there's a flash, and it
  falls. "It's raining outside."
- **8–13 s.** The fall, past the roof's tiles. The drop lands on a leaf (plink), the leaf sags and springs back,
  and the drop slides off and rushes at the camera.
- **13–19 s.** It splats on the glass and runs down in stick-slip steps, swallowing the beads in its path.
  Behind the glass are a desk, a steaming mug, books, a plant and a phone lying face-down. "Your phone can wait."
- **19–25 s.** It hangs from the sill, swells, lets go and lands in the puddle (plink), and the rings spread.
  The rain thins out. "Just focus on what matters."
- **25–30 s.** The sun: light bands, a warm wash, steam rising off the puddle. End card: the real app icon,
  "WildFocus" and "25 minutes. Phone down."
- **Sound.** Rain made in code: pink-noise rain, a brown-noise body, a far rumble at the flash, drips, a plink on each
  landing, and the rain easing as the sun comes. `-silent.mp4` is for adding a TikTok sound in the app.
