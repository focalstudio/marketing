# Focus rabbit: the prompt behind the video

A 30-second vertical video (1080×1920, 30 fps) for WildFocus, drawn in canvas code in the **Kawaii Chibi** look: flat
stickers on white paper, a plum outline, tiny wide-set eyes, pink cheeks, paws and no arms, squash and stretch on every
hop, one saturated green for progress and headings (Nunito Black, charcoal text, no outline or halo). No pocket watch:
the lesson-style progress bar and the 25:00 pill carry the time.

One rabbit's 25-minute focus session, told in 30 s:

- **0-3 s, hook.** The rabbit sits at a small desk (book, mug, phone). Text from frame 0: "This rabbit has **25 minutes** to focus."
  The bar is empty and the pill reads 25:00. The music and the tick-tock start at full level.
- **3-5.5 s.** The phone buzzes and shows a notification ("A new **message.**"). The rabbit is startled: a red "!", wide eyes, ears
  up. It hops, dabs the phone face-down (thunk).
- **5.5-9 s.** "**Phone down.** Timer on." The pill presses (a tap), the bar starts to fill, the clock ticks.
- **9-21 s.** Time-lapse: the pill runs from 25:00 to 00:00, the bar fills, the sun sticker drifts. A butterfly passes
  ("**Not now,** butterfly."); the rabbit glances at it, ignores it and reads on.
- **21-26 s.** 00:00: a chime, the bar flashes, the pill turns green, two big happy hops with confetti. A chibi fox hops in:
  "**25 minutes done.** Someone new moved in."
- **26-30 s.** End card on white: the real WildFocus icon as a sticker, "WildFocus" in green, and a green button
  "25 MINUTES. PHONE DOWN."
- **Sound.** Made in ffmpeg: a bouncy two-bar marimba loop with a bass, clock ticks during the session, a boing per hop, a
  buzz, a thunk, a tap, a chime and sparkles. `-silent.mp4` is for adding a platform sound.
