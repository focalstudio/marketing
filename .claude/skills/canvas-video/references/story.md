# Story

The rules live in [video/STORIES.md](../../../../video/STORIES.md) and [GROWTH.md](../../../../GROWTH.md); this is the short form. Adapted from
HyperFrames' `story.md` and `types/vertical.md`.

- **Hook:** one sentence a person would say, on screen from frame 0, readable within 1 s. No empty opening shot.
- **Premise:** clear by 3 s. If a viewer who left at 3 s could not say what it is about, rework the opening.
- **Beats:** one idea per caption. Captions are short (1-3 lines), large, and never overlap.
- **Ending:** the shared end card, at least 3 s, with `SOUND.close` on its arrival. Nothing else after it.
- **Originality:** animated originals only. Cartoon looks use general traits, never a series' characters or marks.
- **Length:** 15-20 s for Shorts and TikTok. `studio_intro` (45 s, narrated) is the exception, for pinning.
- **Three storylines first:** for a new video, offer three one-line storylines in words only and let the owner pick, then write `<name>.md`.
- **Reference scenes:** `scenes/last25.mjs` (18.5 s, one impact), `scenes/focus_rabbit.mjs` (30 s, UI-lesson look), `scenes/studio_intro.mjs` (45 s, narrated, `BEATS`).
