# Voice

How Focal Studio posts sound. The weekly drafter reads this before writing anything.

## Rules

- Plain words, short sentences. Say what happened, then the number that proves it.
- Real numbers over adjectives: days to ship, PRs merged, roadmap %, revenue, refunds.
- Show the failure too. "What broke" posts earn more trust than launches.
- British spelling (optimise, colour, £).
- One idea per post. No threads longer than 4 posts.
- At most one link per post, and only when it answers something the post raised.
- Every video ends on the same card: icon, WildFocus, "Just focus on what matters.", "Available on iOS" (see `video/STORIES.md`).
- Kit posts talk to developers: name the tool, the file, the command.
- App posts talk to people who'll use the app: no jargon, no stack talk, except the
  "built with Claude Code" line on X and Bluesky.

## Never

- Hype words: "game-changer", "revolutionary", "10x", "unlock", "supercharge", "dive in".
- Emoji walls, or more than 2 hashtags.
- Engagement bait ("Thoughts?", "Who else…?", "Like if…").
- Made-up numbers. If a number isn't in the harvested data, leave it out.
